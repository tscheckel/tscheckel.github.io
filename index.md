I am a PhD student in economics at the University of Salzburg (Austria), advised by Professor Florian Huber. I carry out research at the Joint Research Centre (JRC) of the European Commission in Ispra (Italy). During my PhD, I also completed a research stay at Bocconi University in Milan (Italy), hosted by Professor Massimiliano Marcellino.

My research interests revolve around Bayesian econometrics, multivariate time series analysis and macroeconometrics.
